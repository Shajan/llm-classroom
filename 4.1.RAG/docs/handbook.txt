Vacation. Full-time employees at Brightleaf Robotics get 22 days of paid vacation per year. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year; anything above that is lost on January 31. Vacation requests of more than 3 days must be approved by your manager two weeks in advance.

Remote work. Employees may work from home on Mondays and Fridays. The hardware lab team must be on site Tuesday through Thursday. Working from another country for more than 10 days needs approval from HR because of tax rules.

Expenses. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Flights longer than 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Submit receipts in the Ledgerly app within 30 days; late claims are not paid.

Equipment. New employees choose a laptop on day one: a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon. Laptops are replaced every 3 years. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to it-help@brightleaf.example within 24 hours.

Office. The office is at 410 Harbor Street, building C, third floor. The front door opens with your badge from 7 am to 9 pm. Lunch is catered on Wednesdays, and the robot demo room is open to visitors on the first Friday of each month.

Learning. Every employee has a $1,500 yearly learning budget for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Ask your manager before spending more than $500 at once.
